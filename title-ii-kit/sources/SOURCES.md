## The rule

1. **U.S. Department of Justice, "Accessibility of Web Content and
   Mobile Apps Provided by State and Local Government Entities: A
   Small Entity Compliance Guide," ADA.gov.**
   https://www.ada.gov/resources/small-entity-compliance-guide/
   Primary source for this kit: the WCAG 2.1 Level AA standard, the
   compliance-date table (50,000 or more → April 26, 2027; under
   50,000 and special district governments → April 26, 2028), the
   special-district definition, the school-district population rules,
   the five exceptions and their conditions, equivalent facilitation,
   and the continuing effective-communication and
   reasonable-modification duties.
2. **Final rule** (published April 24, 2024) and **Interim Final Rule
   extending the compliance dates** (published April 20, 2026), both in
   the Federal Register, as cited and linked from the compliance guide
   above. The kit follows the guide's statement of the current dates.
3. **WCAG 2.1, W3C.** https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/ — the technical
   standard the rule names (referenced from the compliance guide).
